FAQ

    
What does each badge mean, and who decides?
      
You decide. __TEST__ Mostly AI: __MEANING_mostly-ai__ Human + AI: __MEANING_human-ai__ Human only: __MEANING_human-only__ __BAR_NOTE__ The badge is a statement from its maker, not a certificate.

    
Is this tracking my visitors?
      
No. It counts places, not people. It stores no IP addresses and sets no cookies.

    
What exactly do you record?
      
The place (site, repo or page address), the badge you chose, and the days it was seen. Nothing about the people who visit.

    
How do I remove my badge or my data?
      
Take the badge off. The next weekly check sees it is gone and deletes its data.

    
Why is there no sign-up?
      
It isn't needed. The badge is the record, and taking it off is how you leave.

    
Can I trust the counter?
      
Each place counts once a day at most. A place counts only after our check finds the badge there: within 15 minutes for a new badge, then again every week. Visits are never counted.

    
Does it work on Substack, GitHub, Medium or Notion?
      
Yes. Paste the page address when you make the badge, and we look for it there within 15 minutes.

    
What about apps, PDFs or videos?
      
Use the plain badge. It works anywhere but is not counted.

    
Is it free? Where is the code?
      
Free. The code is open on GitHub.

    
Can I design a mascot?
      
Yes. Mascots are added on GitHub, and the designer is credited. See how to propose one.

    
Why build this?
      
Badges like "Not By AI" say no machine was involved. This is the mirror: a way to say a machine was involved, and that you are not hiding it.

    
We may remove any place from the public list without notice. It still counts.

Privacy

    
The badge records the place it appears (site, repo or page address), the badge chosen, and the days it was seen. Nothing about the people who visit that place.

    
No cookies, no tracking scripts, no IP addresses stored.

    
The contact form keeps your name, email and message, used only to reply.

    
Turnstile, a Cloudflare service, protects the forms.

    
To delete a badge's data, remove the badge. It is deleted at the next weekly check. For anything else, use the contact form.
